## Priority 4 — Location pages

**My honest recommendation: do not buy stock for these.**

A generic Melbourne skyline on `/solar-panels-melbourne/` is exactly what every
competitor does, it tells a visitor nothing, and Google is not fooled by it.
Location pages rank on genuinely local signals — real jobs in that area, real
suburb names, real customer reviews from there.

**Use your own install photos instead, tagged by city.** One real Geelong roof
beats any amount of Geelong stock. If you have nothing for a city yet, launch
that page without a hero image — I will design a typographic hero that works
without one — and add a photo after the first install there.

If you still want stock as a stopgap, `melbourne suburban houses aerial` and
`sydney suburbs aerial rooftops` are the least generic options.
