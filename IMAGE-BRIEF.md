# Lumenx — image replacement brief

Every image the site uses, what it needs to show, and what to search on Envato
Elements. Save replacements with the **exact filename** listed and drop them in
`public/images/` — nothing in the code needs to change.

**Before you start, read the two notes at the bottom.** One is about alt text
making claims stock photos can't support; the other is about which images
should not be stock at all.

---

## Specs that apply to everything

| | |
|---|---|
| Format to download | JPG at the largest size offered. I convert to WebP. |
| Colour | Warm / golden light suits the brand. Avoid cold blue-grey grading. |
| Avoid | Visible US/EU power poles, non-Australian plugs, snow, obvious American suburbs. |
| Avoid | Heavy lens flare, HDR over-processing, fake-looking composites. |
| People | Australian-looking, natural, not over-posed corporate stock. |

## ⚠ Note 1 — alt text currently claims these are Lumenx installations

Several alt texts say things like *"A family outside their home with rooftop
solar installed by Lumenx"* and *"Lumenx installers fitting solar panels on a
Melbourne rooftop"*. That was written when the plan was real photography.

**Stock images are not your installs, and describing them as such is a false
claim** — a small one, but it sits on a site carrying CEC and NETCC
accreditation, where advertising accuracy is enforceable.

When the new images land, tell me and I will rewrite every alt text on the
stock images to be descriptive rather than attributive — *"A home with rooftop
solar panels"* instead of *"installed by Lumenx"*. The three real photos keep
their attributive alt text, because for those it is true.

## ⚠ Note 2 — licensing

Envato Elements requires a licence registered **per end product**. Register the
licence against the Lumenx website and keep the record — an unregistered
Elements download on a live commercial site is a licence breach, and it is your
agency that carries it, not the client.
